:books: **Code Writers Guild Reading Group — May Session**

This month we're reading *"Programming by Coincidence"* from *The Pragmatic Programmer* — a short chapter that asks a deceptively simple question: does your code work because you *designed* it to, or because you got lucky?

Hunt and Thomas argue that we rely on coincidence more than we'd like to admit — undocumented behavior, untested assumptions, code that happens to work today but could break tomorrow. Come discuss what "coding deliberately" actually looks like in practice.
