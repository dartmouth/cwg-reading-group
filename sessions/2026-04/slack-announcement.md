:books: **Code Writers Guild Reading Group — First Meeting!**

We're kicking off a new monthly discussion group for **anyone at Dartmouth who writes code**. Each month we'll pick a short-ish talk, blog post, or article and talk about how the ideas apply to our work.

For our first session we'll discuss *"All the Little Things"* by Sandi Metz, a fun, opinionated talk about refactoring, small objects, and why duplication beats the wrong abstraction.

If you are interested in good coding practice, want to connect with peers across campus, and are looking to maybe learn a new trick of the trade or two, come join us!
