:books: **Code Writers Guild Reading Group — November Session**

For November, we're watching *"AI Made Me Doubt Everything About Programming"*, a keynote by Felienne Hermans (creator of the Hedy teaching language) from DDD Europe 2026.

Felienne was told spreadsheets aren't "real programming," and found that most popular languages can't read Arabic numerals. She uses those stories, plus glaciers, chess, and Peter Naur, to ask why our field rewards what's hard over what's useful, and whether AI has to take over everything we do. Agree or disagree, it's a great one to argue about.
