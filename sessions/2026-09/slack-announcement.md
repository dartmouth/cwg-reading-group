:books: **Code Writers Guild Reading Group — September Session**

For September, we are reading *"What we talk about when we talk about imposter syndrome"*, and what it means to face the struggle of self-doubt and even feeling like a fraud as a code writer. 

We take a look at some common thoughts amongst developers around imposter syndrome, some of the causes that may spark those feelings, and a few solutions more geared toward institutional and structural change than personal growth and effort.
